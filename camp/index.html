Looking Back

2026 Camp Recap

The 2026 Botswana Camp Meeting was held from 19–26 July under the theme "The Just Shall Live by Faith" (Romans 1:17), drawing believers from Botswana and eight other nations across the region. The week opened with a Workers' Meeting led by Onias Gumbo, setting a tone of consecration that carried through eight days of Bible teaching, evangelistic services, and anointed worship. The children blessed the congregation with their own presentation, the choir ministered throughout the week, and dedicated youth services together with a musical concert stirred hearts across every generation. Seminars on marriage and business equipped saints for practical, everyday faith, deepening the week's call to holy living. The camp closed with visible demonstrations of God's grace — fifteen believers baptized in water, many participating in foot washing, and thirty-six testifying of what they had received from God — leaving the congregation spiritually renewed and inspired to carry the camp's enduring theme into their homes, churches, and daily walk with Christ.
