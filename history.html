The Azusa Street Revival, Los Angeles — 1906

1906

The Beginning of a Worldwide Revival

The roots of the Apostolic Faith Church trace back to the historic revival on Azusa Street in Los Angeles, California. During that revival, countless lives were transformed through salvation, sanctification, and the baptism of the Holy Spirit.

Portland

International Headquarters

Later that same year, Founder Rev. Florence L. Crawford established the Apostolic Faith International Headquarters in Portland, Oregon, USA, where it continues to provide spiritual leadership and doctrinal guidance to churches around the world.

1944

The Gospel Reaches Africa

Through Rev. Timothy G. Oshokoya, the Apostolic Faith Church was officially established in Nigeria, marking the beginning of the work across the African continent.

1955

Southern Africa Opens

Under Rev. Morgan Sengwayo, the Gospel spread into Zimbabwe, laying the spiritual foundation that would eventually reach Botswana.

1968

The Work Begins in Botswana

Rev. Alfred Maseko returned from Zimbabwe after a miraculous healing and spiritual awakening, and began preaching the Gospel in Mopipi and Orapa — planting the seed of what would become today's Apostolic Faith Church Botswana.

Today

Continuing the Mission

Today, the Apostolic Faith Church Botswana continues proclaiming the Gospel through worship, prayer, evangelism, Bible teaching, youth ministries, camp meetings, and missionary outreach, while remaining connected to the International Headquarters in Portland, Oregon.

Our Story in Botswana

How It Began
1968 — Lobatse
Rev. Alfred Maseko — Pioneer & Overseer
The Gospel in Botswana began through Rev. Alfred Maseko. He fell seriously ill while residing in Lobatse and, when local help failed, travelled to Johannesburg seeking treatment. There he met someone whose testimony directed him to Bulawayo, Zimbabwe — where a church would pray for him. He went immediately and was healed, saved, sanctified, and baptized with the Holy Ghost and Fire.
1968 — Mopipi & Orapa
The Gospel Spreads — First Preaching
On returning to Botswana, Rev. Maseko got a job with a construction company which took him to Mopipi and Orapa in the north west, where he began to preach. God blessed his ministry such that in 1970, the very first Apostolic Faith Church building in Botswana was dedicated in Mopipi by Rev. Morgan Sengwayo of Zimbabwe.
1973 — Lobatse
Services in Rev. Maseko's Home
Rev. Maseko was transferred back to Lobatse where services were initially held in his home in Peleng East. As the congregation grew, a small hall was built adjacent to the main house, and church branches began to open across the nation. Rev. Maseko served faithfully as Pioneer and Overseer until 1999.
1990 — Official HQ
Plot 3354, Woodhall 2, Lobatse
A plot was secured and a tabernacle built — becoming the official Headquarters of the Apostolic Faith Work in Botswana.
1999 — New Leadership
Rev. Lordick K. Motshidisi Receives the Torch
Rev. Maseko faithfully passed on the torch to Rev. Lordick K. Motshidisi, who has led the Apostolic Faith Work in Botswana ever since. Rev. Maseko went to be with his Saviour on 26 February 2003.
Our Camp Meetings
Before 2004, Botswana believers did not yet have a dedicated national camp meeting — gatherings took the form of revivals and national Easter meetings held around the country, with many brethren travelling each year to the camp meeting in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe instead.
2004 — First National Camp
Botswana Camp Meeting Begins, Lobatse
The first National Camp Meeting was held in July 2004 in Lobatse, officially opened by the then Southern Africa Overseer, Rev. Richard Sibanda. Attendance was large from the onset, and has grown every year since.
2011 — Gaborone
Camp Ground Relocated to Gaborone
Following completion of the tabernacle in Gaborone, the camp ground relocated there in 2011 — dedicated by Rev. Timothy Kaibula, District Superintendent of Eastern Africa. Camp Meeting has been held in Gaborone every year since.
