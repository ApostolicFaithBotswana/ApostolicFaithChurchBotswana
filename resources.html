Testimony of Our Country Overseer

Rev
Lordick Motshidisi
Botswana Country Overseer
"During the Easter services of 1983, I surrendered my life to Him and experienced salvation. At last, I had peace in my heart and knew I was ready for Heaven."

A Worker for God

Raised in a small village in Botswana, I was not brought up in a Christian home, yet God continued to draw me to Himself. As a young man searching for truth, I found the Apostolic Faith Church while working in Jwaneng. During the Easter services of 1983, I surrendered my life to Christ and experienced salvation. In the years that followed, God sanctified me, baptized me with the Holy Spirit, and opened doors for ministry that I never imagined.

Looking back, I can clearly see God's hand guiding every step of my journey, from providing for my family and career to calling me into full-time ministry as Botswana Country Overseer. My prayer is that every person will experience the same saving grace and discover God's purpose for their life.
