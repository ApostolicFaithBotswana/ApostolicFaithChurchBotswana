Who We Are

A Worldwide Fellowship, Rooted in Botswana

The Apostolic Faith Church in Botswana is a Bible-believing, Christ-centred fellowship and part of a worldwide movement that began at the 1906 Azusa Street Revival in Los Angeles. Our International Headquarters has stood in Portland, Oregon since our founding, and today the Gospel we carry reaches across Africa and the world — united by one Gospel, one doctrine, and one mission.

For a detailed account of how the Gospel journeyed from Azusa Street to Botswana, please visit our History page.

What We Believe

Sound Doctrine

We uphold a Trinitarian and fundamental Christian faith, believing in the divine inspiration of the Bible as God's infallible Word. Following is a summary of our basic doctrines, placing vital emphasis on three definite and separate Christian experiences.

Justification (Salvation)

Salvation is the act of God's grace whereby we receive forgiveness for sins and stand before God as though we had never sinned. "Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins." Acts 2:38

Sanctification

Sanctification, or holiness, is the act of God's grace whereby we are made holy — the second definite work of grace, subsequent to salvation, enabling the believer to live a holy life unto God. "The very God of peace sanctify you wholly." 1 Thess. 5:23

Baptism of the Holy Ghost

The Baptism of the Holy Ghost is the enduement of power from on high upon the clean, sanctified life, evidenced by speaking in tongues as the Spirit gives utterance, empowering believers for witness and service. Acts 2:4

Water Baptism

Water Baptism is by one immersion "in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost," as Jesus commanded — an outward sign of an inward work of grace. Matthew 28:19

Divine Healing

The healing of sickness is provided for through the atonement of Christ Jesus our Lord. James 5:14-16

The Second Coming of Christ

We believe in the literal, bodily return of our Lord Jesus Christ — first to receive His Bride, then to execute judgment and establish His reign. This blessed hope is the anchor of our faith. 1 Thess. 4:16-17
